# Third-party components

The inference release vendors the model definitions needed by the trained
checkpoints:

- Depth Anything V2 / DINOv2 model components under
  `third_party/depth_anything_v2`;
- IGEV-Stereo model components under `third_party/igev_stereo`.

The files are retained to make the released checkpoints directly runnable.
Please consult the corresponding upstream projects for their original papers,
licenses, and attribution requirements before redistribution.
